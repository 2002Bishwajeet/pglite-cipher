pglite-cipher demo

Postgres in this tab, encrypted at rest in IndexedDB. Everything runs locally; the key is generated for this page and never leaves it.

1. Speed

Writes rows to a plain idb:// store and to an encrypted one, then reopens each.

2. Proof it is encrypted

Searches the raw bytes IndexedDB holds for your word and shows them: around the first match in the plain store, the start of a block in the encrypted one.

3. Tamper

Flips one bit in one stored block, then opens the encrypted store again.
